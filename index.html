Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 23, 2026

Welcome to No Unicorns. No Unicorns is an entertainment app designed to provide users with a fun, humorous, and sometimes brutally direct “reality check” based on answers provided during a quiz.

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is used by the app, how quiz information is handled, and how third-party services used by No Unicorns may process technical or advertising-related information.

1. Information You Provide

No Unicorns asks approximately 15 questions about your experiences, interactions, or perception of another person. You may answer questions using options such as Yes, No, or Sometimes.

You may also choose to provide your zodiac sign and the other person's zodiac sign. Zodiac information is optional and may be skipped.

No Unicorns does not require you to provide names, addresses, phone numbers, financial information, Social Security numbers, medical information, passwords, or other highly sensitive identifying information. Please do not enter that type of information into the app.

2. How Quiz Information Is Used

Your quiz selections are temporarily used during your current session so the app can calculate and display your results.

In simple terms, the app has to remember which answers you selected long enough to calculate your results. For example, your combination of Yes, No, and Sometimes answers is used by the app's programmed scoring logic to create your Relationship Snapshot, answers displayed on the Results screen, and Mick's commentary.

This temporary use of your selections does not mean No Unicorns creates a permanent profile about you or the person you are thinking about.

No Unicorns does not independently verify whether the information or answers you provide are accurate.

3. Storage and Deletion of Quiz Responses

No Unicorns is designed to use your quiz selections and generated results temporarily as part of your current quiz session.

No Unicorns does not require an account and does not intentionally create a permanent server-side database record containing your quiz answers.

When you choose to erase or restart your answers, the app clears the quiz information associated with that session so you can begin again.

If you choose not to erase your answers when prompted, the app may keep the current session information temporarily so it can display the results you requested. This does not create a permanent user account or permanent quiz history.

4. Zodiac Information

Providing zodiac information is completely optional. You may skip the zodiac portion of the app and still complete the main questionnaire.

Zodiac compatibility does not change the scoring of your 15-question behavioral quiz. It is displayed separately as an entertainment-based compatibility percentage.

Zodiac and astrology information should not be interpreted as a scientific measurement or factual determination of relationship compatibility.

Depending on Google's services, configuration, device settings, consent choices, and applicable law, this may include information such as IP address, general location derived from an IP address, device identifiers, advertising information, app or ad interactions, diagnostic information, and performance information.

No Unicorns does not provide your answers to the 15-question relationship quiz to Google AdMob for the purpose of selecting advertisements. No Unicorns does not sell your quiz answers to advertisers.

Sharing No Unicorns does not automatically share your quiz answers, Relationship Snapshot, zodiac selections, Mick's commentary, or other private quiz results.

No Unicorns is intended for users 13 years of age and older and is not designed for children under 13.
