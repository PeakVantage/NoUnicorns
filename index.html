About No Unicorns Relationships

No Unicorns Relationships is a relationship-themed entertainment application operated under Peak Vantage LLC.

The app provides a humorous and intentionally direct “reality check” based primarily on answers selected during an approximately 15-question quiz.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled while using the app, what information may be processed by third-party services, and what choices are available to users.

1. Information You Provide During the Quiz

No Unicorns Relationships asks approximately 15 questions about your perception of another person's actions, communication, consistency, support, respect, and related relationship behaviors.

Responses may include options such as Yes, No, or Sometimes.

You may also voluntarily select your zodiac sign and another person's zodiac sign. Zodiac information is optional and may be skipped.

The app does not require you to provide a full name, home address, telephone number, Social Security number, financial information, password, medical information, or other highly sensitive identifying information as part of the quiz.

Please do not enter highly sensitive identifying information into the app.

2. How Quiz Answers Are Used

Quiz selections are temporarily processed during the current app session so No Unicorns Relationships can calculate and display results.

In simple terms, the app must temporarily recognize the answers you select long enough to determine which results should appear.

Your combination of Yes, No, and Sometimes responses may be used by the app's programmed scoring logic to generate:

  • Your Relationship Snapshot;
  • Yes/No result reveals;
  • Category percentages;
  • “Should I Keep Investing in This Person?” commentary; and
  • Mick's entertainment commentary and thoughts.

Mick is the fictional unicorn guide and mascot used throughout No Unicorns Relationships. Mick's comments are generated through programmed entertainment logic based on the quiz results.

No Unicorns Relationships cannot independently verify whether the answers you provide are accurate.

3. Quiz Storage and Session Data

No Unicorns Relationships does not require a user account or login.

Quiz answers and zodiac selections are designed to remain within the app's current session and are not intentionally transmitted to a No Unicorns Relationships server for permanent storage.

The app does not intentionally create a permanent server-side quiz history or permanent relationship profile from your answers.

When a quiz session is erased, reset, replaced, or otherwise cleared, the current quiz selections are intended to be discarded.

Limited non-quiz information may be stored locally on the device when necessary for app functionality. For example, the app may maintain limited local information used to determine when Apple's system review prompt may be requested.

Those local review-related values do not contain your relationship quiz answers or zodiac selections.

4. Zodiac Compatibility

Zodiac information is optional.

Users may skip zodiac selections and still complete the primary relationship questionnaire.

Zodiac compatibility is displayed separately from the behavioral quiz results and does not change the scoring generated from the approximately 15 relationship questions.

Zodiac percentages are provided strictly as an entertainment feature. Astrology and zodiac compatibility should not be interpreted as a scientifically proven method of determining relationship compatibility, personality, intentions, or future relationship success.
