Before You Contact Us

Why can't I press “Let's Get Real” on the Welcome screen?

Before continuing, you must check the box confirming that you have read and agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

Agreeing is your choice, but you cannot continue into the app unless the agreement box is checked.

Why can't I get past the Reality Check screen?

You have two choices: “Show Me the Truth” or “Let Me Live in Denial.” Choosing “Show Me the Truth” continues the quiz.

If you choose “Let Me Live in Denial,” Mick will give you another chance. You can return to the Welcome screen or choose “Wait, I'm Ready” to return and continue.

Who is Mick?

Mick is the fictional unicorn guide and mascot of No Unicorns Relationships.

Mick provides humorous, sarcastic, blunt, snarky, and occasionally judgmental commentary based on the results generated by the app's programmed entertainment logic.

Mick's comments are part of the entertainment experience. They are not professional advice, factual determinations, psychological assessments, or verified statements about you or another person.

My answer didn't register. What should I do?

Try selecting Yes, No, or Sometimes again. If the app still doesn't respond, completely close and reopen No Unicorns Relationships and restart the quiz.

I can't move to the next question. What should I do?

Make sure you've selected an answer for the current question. If you already selected an answer and nothing happens, completely close and reopen the app and try again.

Can I go back and change an answer?

Yes. You can go back to a previous question and change your answer before completing the quiz. Your updated selection will be used when your results are calculated.

How are my results determined?

Your primary results are generated from the Yes, No, and Sometimes responses you select during the approximately 15-question relationship-themed quiz.

The app processes those selections using programmed scoring and entertainment logic to generate your Relationship Snapshot, result reveals, percentages, applicable commentary, and other results.

No Unicorns Relationships cannot actually determine what another person thinks, feels, intends, or will do. Results are provided for entertainment and personal reflection.

How does Mick know what I answered if my answers aren't permanently saved?

Think of it like a calculator. The app temporarily recognizes your selections while you're taking the quiz because it needs those selections to calculate your results.

Your combination of Yes, No, and Sometimes answers tells the app which results and Mick commentary to display.

No permanent No Unicorns Relationships account or intentional permanent server-side quiz history is created from those answers.

What does “Erase My Answers” mean?

It means clearing the answers associated with your current quiz session. The app temporarily uses your selections to calculate and display your results.

If you choose to erase or restart your answers, the current quiz information is cleared so you can begin again.

Does No Unicorns Relationships save my quiz answers?

No Unicorns Relationships does not require an account and is not designed to create a permanent server-side history containing your relationship quiz answers.

Your selections are temporarily used during the current session so the app can calculate and display the results you requested.

Are my quiz answers sent to Google AdMob?

No. No Unicorns Relationships does not provide your relationship quiz answers or zodiac selections to Google AdMob for the purpose of selecting advertisements.

Google advertising technology may separately process certain technical, device, advertising, interaction, or diagnostic information as described in our Privacy Policy and Google's applicable privacy practices.

My results aren't showing. What should I do?

Make sure you've completed all required quiz questions. If your results still don't appear, completely close and reopen the app and begin a new quiz session.

I think No Unicorns Relationships gave me the wrong result.

Results are generated from the answers you selected and the app's programmed scoring rules.

No Unicorns Relationships cannot independently verify your situation or read anyone's mind. Mick has limits. Rather severe ones, being a fictional unicorn and all.

Why did I get a different result this time?

Different answers can produce different results. Even small changes in your Yes, No, or Sometimes selections may change your scores, results, or Mick's commentary.

Do I have to enter my zodiac sign?

No. Zodiac information is completely optional. You can skip that portion and still complete the primary No Unicorns Relationships questionnaire.

Is the zodiac compatibility percentage scientifically accurate?

No. The zodiac compatibility feature is for entertainment only.

No Unicorns Relationships uses programmed compatibility values to provide an astrology-themed comparison, but astrology is not presented as a scientifically proven method of determining whether two people are compatible.

Does my zodiac sign change my main quiz results?

No. Zodiac compatibility is separate from the primary relationship questionnaire.

Your Relationship Snapshot and behavioral results are based on your responses to the approximately 15 quiz questions. Zodiac information provides a separate entertainment-based compatibility percentage.

Who can use No Unicorns Relationships?

No Unicorns Relationships is intended for users 13 years of age and older.
