Support Center

Having trouble with No Unicorns? Check the answers below. If your issue is still being difficult, you can contact us for help.

Before You Contact Us

First, make sure you're using the latest version of No Unicorns. Closing and reopening the app can also solve temporary issues. Technology occasionally needs the digital equivalent of being told to sit down and behave.

Why can't I press “Let's Get Real” on the Welcome screen?

Before you can continue, you must check the box confirming that you have read and agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions. Agreeing is your choice, but you cannot continue into the app unless the box is checked.

Why can't I get past the Reality Check screen?

You have two choices: “Show Me the Truth” or “Let Me Live in Denial.” Choosing “Show Me the Truth” continues the quiz. If you choose “Let Me Live in Denial,” Mick will give you another chance. You can return to the Welcome screen or choose “Wait, I'm Ready” to return and continue.

My answer didn't register. What should I do?

Try selecting Yes, No, or Sometimes again. If the app still doesn't respond, close and reopen No Unicorns and restart the quiz.

I can't move to the next question. What should I do?

Make sure you've selected an answer for the current question. If you already selected an answer and nothing happens, close and reopen the app and try again.

Can I go back and change an answer?

Yes. You can go back to a previous question and change your answer before completing the quiz. Your updated selection will be used when your results are calculated.

How are my results determined?

Your main results come from the Yes, No, and Sometimes answers you select during the approximately 15-question quiz. The app uses those selections with its programmed scoring rules to create your Relationship Snapshot and other results. No Unicorns cannot actually know what another person thinks or feels. The results are for entertainment.

How does Mick know what I answered if my answers aren't permanently saved?

Think of it like a calculator. The app temporarily remembers your selections while you're taking the quiz because it needs them to calculate your result. Your combination of Yes, No, and Sometimes answers tells the app which results and Mick commentary to display. No permanent No Unicorns account or permanent server-side quiz history is created from those answers.

What does “Erase My Answers” mean?

It means erase the answers from your current quiz session. The app has to temporarily use your selections to calculate your results. If you choose to erase them, the current quiz information is cleared so you can start over. If you choose not to erase them yet, the app can keep the current session long enough to show you the results you requested.

Does No Unicorns save my quiz answers?

No Unicorns does not require an account and is not designed to create a permanent server-side history of your quiz answers. Your selections are temporarily used during the current session so the app can calculate and display your results.

My results aren't showing. What should I do?

Make sure you've completed all required quiz questions. If your results still don't appear, completely close and reopen the app and try a new quiz session.

I think No Unicorns gave me the wrong result.

Your results are generated from the answers you selected and the app's programmed scoring rules. No Unicorns cannot verify your situation or read anyone's mind. Mick has limits.

Why did I get a different result this time?

Different answers can produce different results. Even small changes in your Yes, No, or Sometimes selections may change your scores and the commentary you receive.

Do I have to enter my zodiac sign?

No. Zodiac information is completely optional. You can skip that portion and still complete the main No Unicorns questionnaire.

Is the zodiac compatibility percentage scientifically accurate?

No. The zodiac compatibility feature is for entertainment only. No Unicorns uses programmed compatibility values to provide a fun astrology-based comparison, but astrology is not presented as a scientific way to determine whether two people are compatible.

Does my zodiac sign change my main quiz results?

No. Your zodiac compatibility percentage is separate from the main questionnaire. Your Relationship Snapshot and behavioral results are based on your answers to the approximately 15 quiz questions. Zodiac information simply adds a separate entertainment-based compatibility percentage.

Why does No Unicorns have ads?

Ads help support the operation and continued development of No Unicorns. Some optional commentary or features may require you to watch a rewarded ad before they are unlocked. No Unicorns may earn advertising revenue when ads are displayed or interacted with.

Where do the ads come from?

No Unicorns uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements. Google's advertising technology may process certain device, network, advertising, interaction, diagnostic, or performance information as described in the No Unicorns Privacy Policy and Google's own privacy practices. No Unicorns does not provide your relationship quiz answers to AdMob for selecting advertisements.

Do I have to watch an ad?

You can complete the main questionnaire and view the standard results provided by the app without purchasing anything. Certain optional commentary or features may require a rewarded ad to unlock them.

Can I pay for an ad-free version?

Not at this time. An ad-free version of No Unicorns is not currently available, although additional options may be offered in the future.

Can I share No Unicorns with someone else?

Absolutely. If you know someone who could use a reality check of their own, you can use the app's sharing feature to tell them about No Unicorns. Sharing is completely optional.

If I share the app, does it share my answers or results?

No. The standard sharing feature shares information about No Unicorns so your friend can try the app themselves. It does not automatically send them your quiz answers, Relationship Snapshot, zodiac selections, or Mick's commentary.

Is No Unicorns giving me real relationship advice?

No. No Unicorns is for entertainment only. Results, scores, zodiac percentages, and Mick's commentary should not replace your own judgment or professional advice when dealing with serious situations.

Why is Mick so blunt?

That's the point. Mick is a fictional character designed to deliver humorous, sarcastic, blunt, and occasionally judgmental commentary. No Unicorns calls itself a reality check for a reason. His commentary is entertainment, not a factual statement about you or another person.

Who can use No Unicorns?

No Unicorns is intended for users age 13 and older. The app contains relationship topics and intentionally blunt and sarcastic commentary. Users under 13 should not use the app.

The app isn't working correctly after an update. What should I do?

Make sure the latest version finished installing, then completely close and reopen the app. If the problem continues, contact support below.

Should I reinstall the app if something isn't working?

Try closing and reopening the app first. If the issue continues, reinstalling the latest version may resolve problems caused by an incomplete download or update.

Still Need Help?

If you couldn't find your answer above, contact No Unicorns Support. When reporting a problem, briefly explain what happened, what screen you were using, and what you expected to happen.
