Support Center

Having trouble with No Unicorns? Check the answers below. If your issue is still being difficult, you can contact us for help.

Before You Contact Us

First, make sure you're using the latest version of No Unicorns. Closing and reopening the app can also solve temporary issues. Technology occasionally needs the digital equivalent of being told to sit down and behave.

My answer didn't register. What should I do?

Try selecting Yes, No, or Sometimes again. If the app still doesn't respond, close and reopen No Unicorns and restart the quiz.

I can't move to the next question. What should I do?

Make sure you've selected an answer for the current question. If you already have and nothing happens, restart the app and try again.

My results aren't showing. What should I do?

Make sure you've completed all required quiz questions. If your results still don't appear, close and reopen the app and try a new quiz session.

I think No Unicorns gave me the wrong result.

Your results are generated from the answers you selected. No Unicorns cannot verify your situation or read anyone's mind. Mick has limits.

Can I change an answer?

If the current version of the quiz does not allow you to go back, complete or restart the quiz and answer the questions again.

Why am I getting different questions?

No Unicorns may rotate questions between quiz sessions while still measuring the same core areas used to generate your results.

Why did I get a different result this time?

Different answers can produce different results. Even small changes in your responses may change how No Unicorns evaluates the situation.

Do I have to enter my age or zodiac sign?

No. Age and zodiac information are optional and are used only for additional entertainment features when provided.

Does No Unicorns save my quiz answers?

No Unicorns is designed to use your answers during your current quiz session to generate results and does not intentionally create a permanent account or database record of your quiz responses.

Can I share No Unicorns with someone else?

Absolutely. If you know someone who could use a reality check of their own, you can use the app's sharing feature when available.

Is No Unicorns giving me real relationship advice?

No. No Unicorns is for entertainment only. Results and commentary should not replace professional advice or your own judgment.

The app isn't working correctly after an update. What should I do?

Make sure the latest version finished installing, then completely close and reopen the app. If the problem continues, contact support below.

Should I reinstall the app if something isn't working?

Try closing and reopening the app first. If the issue continues, reinstalling the latest version may resolve problems caused by an incomplete download or update.

Still Need Help?

If you couldn't find your answer above, contact No Unicorns Support. When reporting a problem, briefly explain what happened and what screen you were using when the issue occurred.
